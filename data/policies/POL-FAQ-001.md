# POL-FAQ-001: FAQ: what counts as SOC 2 evidence

## Rules

- **POL-FAQ-001.R1** Only a SOC 2 Type II report issued by an independent auditor counts.
- **POL-FAQ-001.R2** Bridge letters do not replace an expired report.
