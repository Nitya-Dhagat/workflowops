# POL-ONB-006: Duplicate partner records

## Rules

- **POL-ONB-006.R1** If two CRM partner records share a domain, the request must be escalated to a data steward.
- **POL-ONB-006.R2** No portal access or external notification may be issued while a duplicate record is unresolved.
