# POL-CMP-001: Compliance document requests

## Rules

- **POL-CMP-001.R1** Requests for missing compliance documents are sent to the internal requester, who coordinates with the partner.
- **POL-CMP-001.R2** A compliance document request is a medium-risk internal action.
