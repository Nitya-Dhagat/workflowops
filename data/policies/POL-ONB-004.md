# POL-ONB-004: Inactive account owners

## Rules

- **POL-ONB-004.R1** If the partner's account owner is inactive, a new active account owner must be assigned before the onboarding task is created.
- **POL-ONB-004.R2** Owner reassignment is a medium-risk internal action.
