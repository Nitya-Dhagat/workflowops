# POL-ONB-007: Restricted regions

## Rules

- **POL-ONB-007.R1** Partners located in restricted regions ZR-1 or ZR-2 must not be onboarded.
- **POL-ONB-007.R2** Requests for such partners are rejected and no actions are executed.
