# POL-ONB-003: Onboarding task ownership

## Rules

- **POL-ONB-003.R1** Every onboarding creates exactly one internal onboarding task in ticketing.
- **POL-ONB-003.R2** The onboarding task is assigned to the partner's account owner, who must be an active user.
