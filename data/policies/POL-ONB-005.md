# POL-ONB-005: External partner communication

## Rules

- **POL-ONB-005.R1** Any external notification to a partner is a high-risk action and always requires human approval.
- **POL-ONB-005.R2** A welcome notification may only be sent after compliance evidence (POL-ONB-002) is satisfied.
