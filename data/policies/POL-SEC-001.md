# POL-SEC-001: Portal access levels

## Rules

- **POL-SEC-001.R1** Automated onboarding may only grant the `standard` portal access level.
- **POL-SEC-001.R2** The `admin` access level is never granted by automation.
- **POL-SEC-001.R3** Granting portal access is a high-risk action and always requires human approval.
