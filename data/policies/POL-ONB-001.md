# POL-ONB-001: Partner onboarding scope

## Rules

- **POL-ONB-001.R1** An onboarding request must reference a partner that exists in the CRM.
- **POL-ONB-001.R2** Partners whose account status is `active` are already onboarded; a new onboarding request for them must be rejected.
- **POL-ONB-001.R3** Only partners with account status `prospect` may be onboarded.
