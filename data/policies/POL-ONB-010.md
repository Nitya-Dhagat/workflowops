# POL-ONB-010: Compliance evidence (legacy)

## Rules

- **POL-ONB-010.R1** A SOC 2 Type II report OR an ISO 27001 certificate is acceptable before portal access is granted.
- **POL-ONB-010.R2** This policy is superseded by POL-ONB-002 and must not be applied.
